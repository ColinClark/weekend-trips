Bad Wildbad Colin's pick

Northern Black Forest
🚗 ~2h 16m · 201 km

🏨 Mokni's Palais Hotel & SPA

Why it fits
  • Long-weekend wellness candidate.
  • Spa town, so less Monday-closure risk than a village.
  • Two thermal baths included with regular room rates: the directly connected historic Palais Thermal (19th-c. bathing halls, sauna world, year-round warm thermal outdoor pool) and the Vital Therme a 2-minute walk away.
  • Rooms split between the 'Badhotel' and 'Rossini' buildings – Rossini serves the Sekt breakfast buffet, Badhotel holds the 200 m² gym.
  • Ask for a quiet room away from lifts.
Regular events
  • Late July – early August (2026: 23 Jul – 2 Aug) – Belcanto Opera Festival 'Rossini in Wildbad'
  • December (2026: 10–13 Dec) – Bad Wildbader Weihnachtsmarkt (Kurpark, Trinkhallenplatz)

Event calendar →

Best time: Year-round (thermal baths); Rossini festival in summer, Christmas market in December

Caveats
  • Adults only (16+)
  • Palais Thermal included on departure day only until 11:00 check-out (afterwards €20 p.p.); Vital Therme not included on departure day

Bacharach

Middle Rhine (UNESCO World Heritage)
🚗 ~36 min · 37 km

🏨 Hotel Altkölnischer Hof

Why it fits
  • Family-run hotel in a historic half-timbered house in the centre, terrace on the market square
  • Hotel mentions a generous breakfast buffet; restaurant serves Rhenish specialities
  • Romantic medieval wine town below Burg Stahleck
Regular events
  • May (next: 22–23 May 2027) – Vierthälermarkt – medieval market on the Rhine promenade (every two years)
  • 4th Saturday in August – Weinfest-Sommernacht with Mittelrhein-Lichter fireworks
  • June (2027: 18–20 Jun) – Steeger Weinblütenfest
  • 2nd Advent weekend (2026: 5–6 Dec) – Schlender-Advent on the historic market square

Event calendar →

Best time: May (medieval market, odd years), late August, 2nd Advent weekend

Caveats
  • Restaurant closed Mondays and Thursdays
  • Only some rooms reachable by lift; hotel parking 150 m away (€6/day)

Oberwesel

Middle Rhine (UNESCO World Heritage)
🚗 ~46 min · 48 km

🏨 Burghotel Auf Schönburg

Why it fits
  • Family-run 4-star castle hotel inside a medieval castle above the town
  • Historic Middle Rhine wine town in the UNESCO World Heritage valley
  • Strong recurring calendar: medieval spectacle at Pentecost, Weinmarkt in September
Regular events
  • Pentecost weekend (2026: smaller Hoffest 23–24 May) – Mittelalterliches Spectaculum ('Esse Vosolvia')
  • September (2026: 10–14 Sep) – Oberweseler Weinmarkt (normally with 'Rhein in Flammen' fireworks)

Event calendar →

Best time: Pentecost, September (Weinmarkt), or autumn

Caveats
  • Winter break mid-Jan to mid-Mar (2027: 15 Jan – 14 Mar); restaurant/museum closed then, only 9 rooms bookable Fri–Sun with breakfast
  • Rhein in Flammen 2026 in Oberwesel was cancelled due to low water

  • Late Oct / early Nov (2026: 30 Oct – 2 Nov) – Kappeskerb & Weinlesefest, Eltville
  • Advent weekends (2026: 5–6 Dec; Eberbach 5–6 & 12–13 Dec) – Eltviller Weihnachtsmarkt & Eberbacher Weihnacht

Event calendar →

Best time: Summer (Rheingau Musik Festival, Sektfest), late October (Kappeskerb), Advent

Traben-Trarbach

Mosel
🚗 ~54 min · 61 km

🏨 Romantik Jugendstilhotel Bellevue

Why it fits
  • Bills itself as Germany's only originally preserved Art Nouveau (Jugendstil) hotel, right on the Mosel
  • Wellness area with penthouse sauna, pool and spa treatments; award-winning restaurant 'Belle Époque'
  • Historic wine town famous for its 16th-c.+ wine cellars ('Unterwelt')
Regular events
  • Late Nov – early Jan, weekends (2026/27: 20 Nov – 2 Jan) – Mosel-Wein-Nachts-Markt – Germany's only underground Christmas market, in historic wine cellars
  • July (2026: 10–13 Jul) – Moselwein-Festival on the Trarbach riverbank, incl. 'Unterwelt' cellar wine tasting

Event calendar →

Best time: Advent (underground Christmas market) or July (Moselwein-Festival); autumn wine season

Caveats
  • Penthouse sauna opens 14:00–21:00 only

Mosel

Why it fits
  • Family-run historic Weinstube hotel in the old town of Bernkastel, near the Mosel

Event calendar →

Best time: Early September (Weinfest) or Advent

Caveats
  • Very busy during the Weinfest der Mittelmosel – book early

Gengenbach

Black Forest (Kinzig valley / Ortenau)
🚗 ~2h 29m · 243 km

🏨 Die Reichsstadt (boutique hotel & spa)

Why it fits
  • Former free imperial town with a half-timbered old town
  • Family-run 4-star boutique hotel in the old town with its own 'Altstadt SPA' and outdoor pool – combines charm + wellness
  • Open year-round
Regular events
  • 30 Nov – 23 Dec (facade visible until 6 Jan) – Gengenbacher Adventskalender – town-hall windows opened daily at 18:00
  • Late Nov – 23 Dec (2026: 27 Nov – 23 Dec) – Gengenbacher Adventsmarkt on the historic market square

Event calendar →

Best time: Advent (town-hall facade turned into a giant Advent calendar, billed as the world's largest) or spring/autumn in the vineyards

Caveats
  • Near the top of the 2.5 h range
  • Town hall is scaffolded in 2026; the Advent-calendar motif is recreated in original size in front of the scaffold
